What gets deleted

Your phone number, your profile name, the full message history for your thread, and any notes or documents the assistant created because of your conversation. Delivery logs are removed. We keep one record that the request was made and completed, so we can prove it.

What we cannot delete

Copies held by the messaging platform itself (Meta, Telegram) are governed by their own policies; deleting your WhatsApp chat on your own device removes your copy. Aggregated counters that no longer identify anyone are kept.

Requests are actioned within 30 days, usually within a few days. If you have not heard back in that window, write again and quote the date you first asked.
