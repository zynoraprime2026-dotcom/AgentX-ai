Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026 · AgentX, operated by Al‑Haqq Digital, Tarkwa, Ghana

AgentX is an automated assistant reachable on WhatsApp, Telegram and Messenger, and through a web dashboard. This policy explains what it collects, why, how long it is kept, and how you can have it removed.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your phone number, and your WhatsApp or Telegram profile nameTo answer your messages and keep each conversation separate
The content of messages you send to the assistantTo understand the request and reply; recent turns are replayed so the conversation keeps its context
Technical delivery data (message ids, timestamps, delivery status)To confirm a message arrived and to diagnose failures
Dashboard account details, if you are invited to the dashboard (name, email, hashed password)To sign you in. Passwords are stored only as a hash

We do not ask for payment data, and the assistant does not read your other chats, contacts, photos or files.

Who processes it

Messages travel through Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API (or Telegram) and are stored in a managed PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon. Replies are generated with AI model providers (Groq and, when needed, OpenAI), which receive the text of the conversation needed to answer. Hosting is on Vercel. Each provider processes the data under its own terms. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your messages for advertising.

How long we keep it

Conversation messages are kept while the thread is in use and are trimmed to the most recent messages needed for context. Delivery logs are kept for up to 90 days. Dashboard documents you create stay until you delete them. Anything you ask us to delete is removed within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

Your rights

You may ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, correct it, or have it deleted. Write to the address below and we will confirm within 30 days. You may also stop writing to the assistant at any time, which ends all further collection from you.

Deleting your data

Step-by-step deletion instructions are on /data-deletion. Deletion requests are honoured within 30 days, and the assistant's message history for your number is erased.

Children

AgentX is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If a child has messaged the assistant, write to us and the thread will be removed.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access tokens are stored as environment secrets, dashboard sessions expire, and administrative routes require an authenticated owner.

Automated answers

Replies are generated automatically and may be imperfect, including quoted Islamic material. Verify anything consequential against a named source or a qualified scholar.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above changes and the new version appears at this address.
